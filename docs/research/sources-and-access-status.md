## Numerical corrections and interpretation notes

- Cuperman & Ickes Table 5 is printed p.680. The earlier handoff's p.679 locator is superseded.
- Naumann neatness/criterion conscientiousness r=.18 is nonsignificant; neatness/criterion likability is .17 in the checked table, also nonsignificant. The .53 figure is observers' conscientiousness impressions. These are different columns/outcomes. No recommendation depends on the earlier .17/.18 rounding discrepancy.
- Borkenau's .43 cross-situational consistency is not trait criterion accuracy. Use raw, not disattenuated, Table 5 correlations for the comparisons reported here.
- UDIVA MSE, rapport AP, Pearson r, concordance, β, B, and p-values are different metrics. None directly reports individual conversation success probability.
- Hayashi preprint training-seed standard deviations are not participant-based confidence intervals. Relationship-specific prediction and overall prediction differ.
- Reverdy's held-out dyadic models did not beat baseline. Preserve that result alongside favorable associations.
- Earlier broad initial-liking and romantic-selection results are not renamed conversation satisfaction results.
